# Learn English after chat

Turn a conversation into a Markdown learning resource with Traditional Chinese explanations and natural American English alternatives for your own messages.

## Use after a conversation

In the conversation you want to learn from, ask your agent to read the skill and prepare the document:

```text
Read and follow /path/to/skills/skills/learn-english-after-chat/SKILL.md.

Turn this conversation into an English-learning document.
Save it as english-after-chat.md in the current workspace.
```

Replace `/path/to/skills` with the location of your clone. Specify a topic, range of messages, or output path when you want a narrower selection or a different destination.

## What you get

- Your selected messages preserved verbatim, including mistakes and original line breaks.
- Traditional Chinese explanations of meaningful differences in grammar, word choice, tone, and sentence structure.
- Natural American English versions that preserve your intent, including English ways to express messages originally written in Chinese.
- Relevant assistant responses in their original language to preserve context, clearly labeled when shortened.
- A standalone `.md` file linked in the agent's response.

Already-natural wording is identified as such; alternatives need not imply that the original was wrong. Later feedback can be applied consistently across the document.

## Conversation availability

Use the skill in the thread containing the conversation. It works with the messages actually available to the agent. If earlier content has been summarized or is unavailable, the document should disclose that limitation instead of reconstructing exact quotations.
